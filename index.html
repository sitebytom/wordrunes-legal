Privacy Policy

WordRunes is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

Data Controller: gamebytom. Contact: supportbytom@gmail.com

Data We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and display name. Used for authentication and progress sync.
  • Game Progress: Puzzles completed, scores, achievements, creatures, currency balances, and quest progress. Synced across your devices via our servers.
  • Device Locale: Your device language setting is read to provide translated content.
  • Ad Identifiers: Google AdMob may collect device advertising IDs to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • Accelerometer Data: Read locally for a subtle parallax tilt effect. This data never leaves your device.

Data NOT Collected

  • We do not collect analytics events, crash reports, or installation identifiers.
  • Your app version and device type are read locally but never sent to our servers.

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the game, sync progress across devices, and maintain leaderboards.
  • To serve ads via Google AdMob (you can remove ads permanently via in-app purchase).
  • To process purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (we never see your payment details).
  • To respond to support inquiries.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with essential service providers:

  • Supabase — cloud hosting and database provider.
  • Google AdMob — serves ads. AdMob operates under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process all payments and subscriptions.

Your Rights

  • Access: Download your data at Settings > Account > Download My Data (JSON format including profile, puzzles, and stats).
  • Deletion: Delete your account at Settings > Account > Delete Account. Confirmed via biometrics or device PIN.
  • Retention: Data is retained until you delete your account. Idempotency keys are auto-cleaned after 30 days.
  • Contact: For questions or requests, email supportbytom@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
